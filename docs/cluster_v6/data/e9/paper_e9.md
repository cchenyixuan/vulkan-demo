## 5. tab:ledger3d (ideal chain = the whole case / K; four terms + r)

Columns: case | particles per device (M) | P (ms) | eta | seam (%) | transport (%) | imbalance (%) | r. From the chain trace of each point: P = the step period (mean over the steady steps and cards); ideal = tau(N) / K with tau(N) = the whole case's K = 1 trace step time (128M: the K = 2 pair trace / 4; weak: the 8M cube's K = 1 trace, not divided); seam = (busy - ideal) / P with busy = T_A + T_B + T_C per card, mean over the cards; transport = the exposed transfer, per card and step the largest (t_tr - T_B)+ over its incoming links, mean over steps and cards; imbalance = (P - busy - exposed) / P; r = t_tr / T_B of the worst link per step, median. eta in the table is the timed one (the trials' mean); the trace's own eta' = ideal / P is listed in the check below.
