## 1. Ledger: the chain period split into five terms (% of the period; E8 K = 8 points, chain traces)

P, busy, exposed and the taus are means over the kept steady steps (defrag step dropped); ideal = tau_pc / P with tau_pc = the per-card load N / K at the per-card-size cube's K = 1 cost (that cube's K = 1 trace step time x (N / K) / its particles; the factor is 1 where the cube is the per-card size: 32M -> 4M, 64M -> 8M, 128M -> 16M; the stand-ins scale the 4M / 8M cube's per-particle cost); seam = busy - tau_pc; reference working point = tau(N) / K - tau_pc (sign kept; tau(N) = the whole cube's K = 1 trace, 128M: the pair trace / 4; weak: the 8M cube itself, not scaled); exposed = per card and step the largest (t_tr - T_B)+ over its incoming links, mean over steps and cards; imbalance = P - busy - exposed. Identities: ideal + seam + exposed + imbalance = 100; eta_trace = ideal + reference working point; the five add up to 100 + reference working point.
