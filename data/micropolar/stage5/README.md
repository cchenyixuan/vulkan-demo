# Stage 5 data (docs/micropolar_design.md, Section 14)

The wall rule in its slip form, the exact Stokes benchmark of the Newtonian flow between eccentric rotating
cylinders, and the candidates for the wall and the density-diffusion term. Solver: tag `thesis-ch5-rc1` plus two
options added as compile-time variants during the stage (`numerics.delta_fluid_only`, `numerics.wall_density`);
the default path is byte-identical, and every `run.json` records its commit and the tree hashes of
`experiment/v7/shaders` and `experiment/v7/utils`.

Settings (Newtonian and micropolar, thesis configuration otherwise):

| prefix | setting | case key |
|---|---|---|
| `thesis_`, and the unprefixed runs of Stage 4 | the thesis configuration | none |
| `nd_` | density diffusion off | `numerics.use_density_diffusion: false` |
| `fd_` | density diffusion between fluid pairs only | `numerics.delta_fluid_only: true` |
| `ew_` | adami walls store the Tait density of their pressure | `numerics.wall_density: eos` |

| file | what | made by |
|---|---|---|
| `rule_slip_form.txt` / `.json` | D of the rule in its slip form beside the `(R1/R2)^2` form: the sweep of Stage 4, the host scan over m, a rotor that carries `w = f Omega` (14.1) | `python -m experiment.validation.micropolar_stage5 rule --out data/micropolar/stage5/rule_slip_form.txt` |
| `plane_film.txt` / `.json` | the plane film between two walls with w = 0: exact film function against the rule's, checked against a numerical solution of the model's two ordinary differential equations; the journal bearing in the thin-film approximation with both (14.2) | `micropolar_stage5 film --out ...` |
| `eccentric_reference_check.txt` | the closed form of the Stokes flow between eccentric rotating cylinders and its three checks (14.3.1) | `python -m experiment.validation.micropolar_eccentric_reference check --out ...` |
| `benchmark.json` / `.txt` | every eccentric run of the stage against the exact values: load, torque by the wall and the circle form, steadiness, velocity into the walls against the wall pressure, fluid behind a wall, density and pressure level; the rule between eccentric cylinders; the candidates (14.2, 14.3.2, 14.4.3) | `micropolar_stage5 benchmark --json data/micropolar/stage5/benchmark.json` (needs the raw runs) |
| `concentric.json` / `.txt` | the concentric control of each candidate and the micropolar runs of the pilot with it (14.4.3) | `micropolar_stage5 concentric --json ...` (needs the raw runs) |
| `cavity.json` / `.txt` | the lid-driven cavity at Re = 1000, n = 250 and 500, for each candidate: errors against Marchi et al. 2021, the points nearest the lid and the right wall, velocity into the walls, density (14.4.3) | `python -m experiment.validation.micropolar_stage5_cavity analyse --json data/micropolar/stage5/cavity.json` (needs the raw runs) |
| `couette/<run>/` | the samples of each run of the stage summed over eight windows (`windows.npz`) and its `run.json` | `micropolar_couette reduce --runs logs/micropolar/stage5 --out data/micropolar/stage5/couette <runs>` |
| `wall_sums/<run>.json` | pair sums of both walls and of the circles at distance h, as in Stage 4 | `micropolar_wall_torque.run_sums` (kept beside each raw run as `wall_sums.json`) |
| `t1_k{1,2}_compare*.json` | T1 with the default after each of the two options: five runs against the base repeats, K = 1 and K = 2 | `micropolar_tests dump` / `compare` |
| `seam_{fd,ew}_k{2,3}.json` | per-particle seam check of each option, K = 2 (XTX + 5090, and one card) and K = 3 (one card) | `micropolar_tests seam --pressure` |

The raw runs are under `logs/micropolar/stage5/` (cylinders) and `logs/micropolar/stage5_cavity/` (cavity), which
are not in the repository; the thesis-configuration cavity runs are those of 2026-11-11 under
`logs/micropolar/cavity_forms/`. Case files: `cases/couette_stage5/` and `cases/stage5_cavity_n{250,500}_{nd,fd,ew}/`
(the particle files `*.obj` are regenerated by `micropolar_stage5 queue`; the cavity cases refer to the particle
files of `cases/lid_driven_cavity_2d_n{250,500}`).
