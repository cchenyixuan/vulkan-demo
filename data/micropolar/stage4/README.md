# Stage 4 data (docs/micropolar_design.md, Section 13)

Concentric-cylinder production runs (resolution study, sweep over K and m), the wall sums, the eccentric pilot.
Solver: tag `thesis-ch5-rc1`; every `run.json` records its commit and the tree hashes of `experiment/v7/shaders` and
`experiment/v7/utils`.

| file | what | made by |
|---|---|---|
| `couette_stage4.json` / `.txt` | fits, D against the exact solution and the displacement rule, profile errors, wall offsets, orders, the profiles themselves; every concentric run | `python -m experiment.validation.micropolar_stage4 analyse --runs data/micropolar/stage4/couette --json data/micropolar/stage4/couette_stage4.json` |
| `couette/<run>/` | the samples of each run summed over eight windows (`windows.npz`) and its `run.json` | `micropolar_couette reduce --runs logs/micropolar/stage4 --out data/micropolar/stage4/couette <runs>` |
| `wall_sums/<run>.json` | pair sums of both walls and of the circles at distance h, by term, mean and scatter over the saved states, with the two checks against the GPU | `micropolar_wall_torque.run_sums` (kept beside each raw run as `wall_sums.json`) |
| `wall_torque.json` / `.txt` | the three readings of the wall torque against the torque of the fits (Section 13.3) | `micropolar_stage4 torque --json ...` (needs the raw runs with their states) |
| `eccentric.json` / `.txt` | eccentric pilot: torque and force of both walls, noise floor, D_T, the map of delta_b around the rotor | `micropolar_stage4 eccentric --json ...` (needs the raw runs) |
| `rule_against_exact.txt` | D_rule / D_exact over m on the host (no run) | `micropolar_stage4 rule-scan --out ...` |
| `make_figures.py`, `figure_a_profiles.*`, `figure_b_torque.*` | the two draft figures and the script that makes them from `couette_stage4.json` | `python data/micropolar/stage4/make_figures.py` |

The raw runs (samples of every 1,000th step, 17 to 24 wall states each) are under `logs/micropolar/stage4/`, which
is not in the repository; the case files are under `cases/couette_stage4/` (the particle files `*.obj` are
regenerated by `micropolar_stage4 queue`).
